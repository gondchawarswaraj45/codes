About the Movie

Kaho Naa... Pyaar Hai is a romantic film directed by Rakesh Roshan. It features Hrithik Roshan and Ameesha Patel in the lead roles.

The movie was released on 14 January 2000. It became famous for its songs, romance, and memorable performances.

Genre: Romance, Musical, Thriller
Language: Hindi
Release Year: 2000

This movie was Hrithik Roshan's second debut film.
